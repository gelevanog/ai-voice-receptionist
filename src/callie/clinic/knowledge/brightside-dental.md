# Brightside Dental: front-desk knowledge base

Fictional clinic for the Callie demo. Each section is one retrievable chunk; keep answers short enough to say on the phone.

## Opening hours
We are open Monday, Tuesday and Thursday from 8 AM to 5 PM, Wednesday from 8 AM to 7 PM, Friday from 8 AM to 4 PM, and Saturday from 9 AM to 1 PM. We close for lunch from noon to 1 PM on weekdays. We are closed on Sundays, on Thanksgiving, on Christmas Day and on New Year's Day.

## Location and directions
We are at 418 Linden Street, Suite 2, in Millbrook, on the second floor above the Linden Street pharmacy. The building has an elevator. The number 12 bus stops right outside at the Linden and Fifth stop.

## Parking
There is free parking for patients in the lot behind the building; the entrance is on Fifth Avenue. Street parking on Linden Street is metered for two hours.

## Services
We offer cleanings and checkups, children's cleanings, new patient exams with x-rays, fillings, teeth whitening, crowns, implant consultations, and same-week urgent visits for tooth pain. We do not do orthodontics or braces; we refer those to a partner orthodontist.

## Prices without insurance
Without insurance, a cleaning and checkup is $120, a children's cleaning is $85, a new patient exam with x-rays is $180, a filling starts at $150 per tooth, teeth whitening is $350, an urgent visit for tooth pain is $95, and a crown or implant consultation is free. Exact treatment costs are confirmed by the dentist after the exam.

## Insurance
We are in network with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna and Guardian PPO plans. We also accept most other PPO plans as out of network and file the claim for you. We do not accept HMO dental plans or Medicaid. Please bring your insurance card to your first visit.

## Payment options
We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, HSA and FSA cards, and CareCredit. Payment is due at the time of the visit. Payment plans are available for treatments over $500.

## New patients
New patients are welcome. Please arrive 15 minutes early to fill in a short health history form, and bring a photo ID and your insurance card. Your first visit is usually a new patient exam with x-rays, which takes about an hour.

## Cancellation policy
Please give us at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations and missed appointments may be charged a $40 fee. You can cancel or reschedule by phone at any time, including with Callie, our AI assistant.

## Dental emergencies
For severe tooth pain, a broken tooth or a lost filling, we keep same-week urgent visit slots. If you have swelling in your face or neck, trouble breathing or swallowing, bleeding that does not stop, or an injury to your jaw, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room right away.

## Children
We see children from age three. A parent or guardian must come with patients under 18. Children's cleanings take about 45 minutes.

## Accessibility and languages
Our office is wheelchair accessible. Our team speaks English and Spanish. We can arrange an interpreter for other languages if you ask when you book.

## Appointment reminders
We send a text message to confirm your booking, and a reminder text the day before your appointment. Reply STOP to stop the texts.

## Teeth whitening
In-office whitening takes about 90 minutes and costs $350. We recommend a cleaning before whitening. Whitening is not covered by insurance.

## Contact
Our phone number is 555-010-2040. Callie, our AI assistant, answers the phone around the clock; the front desk team is available during opening hours.
